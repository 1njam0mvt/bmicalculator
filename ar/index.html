    إجابة سريعة

    Reviewed 21 Nov 2026

    مؤشر كتلة الجسم = الوزن (كجم) ÷ الطول (م)². المؤشر الصحي للبالغين من 18.5 إلى 24.9 كجم/م² (منظمة الصحة العالمية): أقل من 18.5 نحافة، و25–29.9 وزن زائد، و30 فأكثر سمنة. للأعمار 2–20 يعتمد CDC على النسب المئوية للمؤشر حسب العمر. تعتمد إرشادات آسيوية كثيرة حدودًا أقل: وزن زائد من 23 وسمنة من 25.

    Health risks

    If overweight

    Higher risk of hypertension, type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, stroke, sleep apnea and osteoarthritis (CDC).

    If underweight

    Higher risk of malnutrition, anemia, osteoporosis and weaker immune function. Unexplained low weight deserves a doctor's visit.

    Limits of BMI

    BMI measures weight relative to height, not body fat. It cannot tell muscle from fat, ignores where fat sits, and reads differently by age, sex and ethnicity: older adults and women carry more fat at the same BMI, while athletes may be classed overweight with little fat.

    Treat it as a screening signal alongside waist size and clinical advice.

    How to work out your BMI

    US units: 5'10", 160 lb

    BMI = 703 × 160 ÷ 70² = 112,480 ÷ 4,900 = 22.96 ≈ 23.0

    Metric: 178 cm, 72.5 kg

    BMI = 72.5 ÷ 1.78² = 72.5 ÷ 3.168 = 22.9

    BMI Prime

    BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25 = 22.9 ÷ 25 = 0.92

    Ponderal Index

    PI = kg ÷ m³ = 72.5 ÷ 1.78³ = 72.5 ÷ 5.640 = 12.9 kg/m³
